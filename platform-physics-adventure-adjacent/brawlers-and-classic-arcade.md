# Brawlers and Classic Arcade Platformers

Godot repositories for beat-'em-up games and classic Atari-era arcade platformers.

Last reviewed: 2026-06-20.

## Beat-'em-ups and Classic Single-Screen

| Repository | Inspired by | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [Anatolij-Grigorjev/StreetsOfDynasty](https://github.com/Anatolij-Grigorjev/StreetsOfDynasty) | Streets of Rage | Beat-'em-up tribute/reference project. |
| [sebastiandine/GodotKong](https://github.com/sebastiandine/GodotKong) | Donkey Kong | Godot Engine rebuild of the classic Donkey Kong arcade game's first level. |

## Pitfall

| Repository | Notes |
|---|---|
| [si-lord/pitfall2-godot](https://github.com/si-lord/pitfall2-godot) | Pitfall 2: Lost Caverns remake in Godot 4.3 (GDScript, MIT). Implements all 255 screens across four zones (Grasslands, Waterways, Caves, Jungle), vine swinging, ladder climbing, quicksand, seven enemy types, collectibles, and a modern pixel-art visual overhaul. `project.godot` present. |
| [YeyoGSD/Pitfall-Practice-Project](https://github.com/YeyoGSD/Pitfall-Practice-Project) | Pitfall! practice project from the 20 Games Challenge, built in Godot with GDScript and C# (GPL-3.0). Implements player movement, ladder climbing, rope/vine mechanics, and hazard pits. Work in progress. |
