# Turn-Based Roguelikes

Godot repositories for grid-based, turn-by-turn roguelikes in the tradition of NetHack, Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup, and classic RL games.

Last reviewed: 2026-06-20.

## Playable Games

| Repository | Notes |
|---|---|
| [Bozar/OneMoreLevel](https://github.com/Bozar/OneMoreLevel) | Turn-based roguelike game made with Godot Engine; playable and complete. 45★. |
| [Bozar/GodotRoguelikeDemo](https://github.com/Bozar/GodotRoguelikeDemo) | Roguelike demo made with Godot Engine; companion project to the tutorial. 39★. |
| [statico/godot-roguelike-example](https://github.com/statico/godot-roguelike-example) | Unfinished Godot 4 sci-fi roguelike with BSP dungeon generation, field of vision, inventory, and behavior trees. 20★. |
| [PavelProvotorov/Godot-Terminus-Roguelike](https://github.com/PavelProvotorov/Godot-Terminus-Roguelike) | Coffee-break roguelike in Godot with pixel-art and turn-based combat. 11★. |
| [Taifuun1/Amulet-Of-Iovar](https://github.com/Taifuun1/Amulet-Of-Iovar) | NetHack-inspired roguelike made in Godot. 8★. |
| [Mizar999/godot-roguelike](https://github.com/Mizar999/godot-roguelike) | Coffee-break roguelike made with Godot Engine. 6★. |
| [Lilith-In-Starlight/Moloch](https://github.com/Lilith-In-Starlight/Moloch) | Wandcrafting roguelike where health is not health points; unusual mechanics. 17★. |
