# Resources for Godot Arcade Games

Free and royalty-free asset sources plus practical Godot tools for building arcade-style games.

Last reviewed: 2026-06-18.

Always check the license on the exact asset, track, sound, or plugin version you download. Many sites mix CC0, CC-BY, custom royalty-free terms, and paid/pro tiers.

## Royalty-Free Music Sites

| Resource | Best for | License notes |
|---|---|---|
| [Pixabay Music](https://pixabay.com/music/) | Fast background music search for prototypes, trailers, menus, and casual games. | Pixabay advertises royalty-free music with no attribution required, but still review the Content License for redistribution limits. |
| [Incompetech](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/music.html) | Kevin MacLeod tracks, including many game-friendly loops and genre cues. | Free Creative Commons option generally requires attribution; paid licenses are available. |
| [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/) | Game-specific music loops and packs across many licenses. | License varies per upload; useful because it also carries sprites, SFX, and attribution metadata. |
| [Mixkit Music](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-music/) | Short free music tracks for menus, trailers, and videos. | Mixkit has item-type-specific licenses; confirm the music license for game use. |
| [Free Music Archive](https://freemusicarchive.org/) | Large independent music catalog. | License varies by track, often Creative Commons. Filter carefully. |
| [Bensound](https://www.bensound.com/royalty-free-music) | Polished production music for menus, trailers, and commercial-style presentation. | Free and paid licensing terms differ; check attribution and usage limits. |
