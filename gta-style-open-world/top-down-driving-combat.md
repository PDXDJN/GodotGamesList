# Top-Down Driving and Combat Components

Godot 4 repos covering overhead vehicle physics and top-down shooting mechanics — the two systems needed to build a GTA1-style game in Godot from scratch.

Last reviewed: 2026-06-20.

## Vehicle Physics

| Repository | Notes |
|---|---|
| [37Rb/godot-overhead-car-2d](https://github.com/37Rb/godot-overhead-car-2d) | Adds `OverheadCarBody2D` for Godot 4, extending `CharacterBody2D` with realistic overhead-view car steering, drift, and friction. The closest ready-made Godot 4 component to GTA1-style vehicle handling. |

## Combat and Shooter Systems

| Repository | Notes |
|---|---|
| [quiver-dev/top-down-shooter-core](https://github.com/quiver-dev/top-down-shooter-core) | Godot 4 top-down shooter template with weapons, inventory, interactables, animated sprites, and custom resources. Solid starting point for the on-foot combat side of a GTA-style project. |
| [josephmbustamante/Godot-Top-down-Shooter-Tutorial](https://github.com/josephmbustamante/Godot-Top-down-Shooter-Tutorial) | End result of a Godot top-down shooter tutorial series. Older and less plug-and-play than the Quiver template, but the GDScript is readable and instructive for understanding the basics. |
